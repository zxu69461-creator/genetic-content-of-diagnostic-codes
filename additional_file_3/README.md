# Aggregate data and selected analytical code

This archive accompanies Measuring the genetic content of diagnostic codes: an ankylosing spondylitis-related signal in seronegative rheumatoid arthritis. It supplies public aggregate extracts, the 43 supplementary tables in a machine-readable format, selected numerical estimators, and executable checks of the principal atlas summaries, AS-locus sensitivity and targeted revision analyses.

## What can be reproduced here

From this directory, run `python code/reproduce_core_results.py`. The script verifies SHA-256 checksums, recomputes the 1677-endpoint HLA ratios and screening counts, checks the headline HLA estimate, and recomputes the external 22-locus and expanded-MHC 21-locus AS-equivalent estimates with delete-one-locus working intervals. It compares the estimates with the supplied numerical results and reports the unsuccessful planned criteria. It does not require network access or the original project database. Dependency versions used during validation are recorded in provenance/validation_environment.json. The requirements file gives compatible minimum versions, not a complete locked environment.

For the targeted analyses, run `python revision/code/analyze_revision.py` followed by `python revision/code/verify_revision.py`. They use only frozen inputs and aggregate LD matrices, writing fresh output to revision/recomputed without changing the supplied evidence. The first recomputes all fixed-panel regressions, chromosome/locus influence, conditional null simulations, HLA covariance sensitivity and the decision summaries of the genome-wide model check (internal label G5); the second independently checks weighted-regression calculations and LD-matrix properties. These commands do not rerun the original genome-wide G5 fit. Its completed outputs and all four unsuccessful decision records are supplied without changing the recorded criteria.

The revision plan was fixed before the new targeted extraction, but after earlier study results. It is not prospective registration. A separate source-scale clarification records that IGAS effects are used in their released units and are not assumed to be clinically commensurate with target logistic effects. Publication coefficients are not disease fractions. The UK Biobank M06.0 targeted result is inconclusive (two-sided P = 0.088); source data and alternative uncertainty estimates are retained.

## Contents and data conventions

`tables/` contains the final supplementary tables; `table_index.json` maps table numbers and titles. `inputs/` contains small aggregate inputs and derived results used in the study. `provenance/input_index.json` records the original extract and publication-copy hashes, row counts and columns. Publication column names and descriptive strings were clarified without changing numerical observations. Historical variable names such as pi, fraction, decoy, bound or recognition elsewhere in code and data are shorthand for model coefficients or specified sensitivity calculations; they do not denote clinically validated disease proportions, individual predictions, diagnostic specificity or identified patient-level bounds. The manuscript and supplementary methods provide the operative definitions.

Blank cells are missing values, not zeros. Very small probabilities are retained at stored precision. Zero probabilities in source results can arise from floating-point underflow or finite bootstrap resolution and should not be interpreted as exact probabilities of zero. The unstable secondary polyarthrosis result is retained and labelled; it is not an interpretable biological loading. Atlas multiplicity summaries and the expanded-MHC sensitivity were added post hoc and are distinct from the recorded historical screen.

Public acquisition URLs, available checksums, counts and acquisition times are in `provenance/public_acquisition_records.tsv`. Blank source checksums mean unavailable, not verified. Internal plan hashes confirm file integrity at the audit; they do not independently establish prospective registration or result blindness. Local acquisition records are not evidence that the remote resource remains unchanged. The selected numerical functions preserve the original computation while omitting development prose; code provenance and the selected function list are provided.

## Scope and access

All included observations are aggregate public summary data; no individual-level genotypes or patient records are supplied. Public reference genotypes at the frozen loci were used to calculate the included European and Finnish-subset LD matrices, but the individual genotypes are not redistributed. This compact archive is not the complete genome-wide analysis environment: full source summary files, reference genotype files and external R toolchains are not redistributed, and all genome-wide analyses were not independently rerun during manuscript preparation. Obtain those resources from the original providers under their current access and reuse terms. Source attribution and cohort publications are in the manuscript and Table S1. The analytical code in `code/` and `revision/code/` is released under the MIT License (see LICENSE). Third-party data and summary statistics remain under their providers' terms; no new license is asserted over them. No persistent repository DOI is claimed for this supplementary archive.

## Interpretation

AS-equivalent signals are normalized genetic contrasts. Converting them into patient fractions requires transportability and mixture assumptions that are not established here. HLA binomial errors, participant overlap, endpoint dependence, physical locus thinning and model identifiability constrain the intervals and conclusions. This archive does not establish individual diagnosis, causation or clinical effectiveness.
